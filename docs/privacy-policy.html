Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Cricket Cards Royale ("the App") is a cricket card game developed by Ijaz Ahamed ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used for account authentication and password recovery
  • Username — chosen by you, displayed to other players during matches and on leaderboards

You may also play as a Guest without providing an email. Guest accounts are bound to your device and persist locally.

Game Data

We automatically collect gameplay data to provide the game experience:

  • Match results (wins, losses, runs, wickets, stat selections)
  • Card collection and roster preferences
  • Experience points, level, and division progression
  • Coin balance and in-app purchase history

Purchase Information

If you make in-app purchases, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (on iOS) or by Google through Google Play (on Android). We receive a transaction confirmation but do not collect or store your payment details, credit card number, or billing information.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Device identifiers for advertising (no IDFA)
  • Health or fitness data
  • Browsing history

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide and operate the game
  • Authenticate your account
  • Display your username and stats to other players during matches and on leaderboards
  • Process in-app purchase coin grants
  • Send password reset emails (only when you request them)

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Your username and match statistics may be visible to other players during multiplayer matches, in friend lists, and on leaderboards. No other personal information is shared.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on cloud servers with industry-standard encryption. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and are never stored in plain text. We use JWT tokens for session management.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • MongoDB Atlas — for data storage (subject to MongoDB's Privacy Policy)
  • Apple App Store Server Notifications — for in-app purchase verification on iOS (subject to Apple's Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Billing — for in-app purchase verification on Android (subject to Google's Privacy Policy)

7. Children's Privacy

The App is rated for ages 9+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us to have it removed.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — viewable in your profile within the App
  • Delete your account — available in Settings > Delete Account, which schedules permanent removal of all your data within 30 days
  • Correct your information — you can update your username in the App

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days. You can cancel deletion by signing back in within that 30-day window.

10. Disclaimer

Cricket Cards Royale is an independent fan-made stat-comparison card game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the BCCI, ICC, IPL, any cricket board, league, franchise, broadcaster, or any cricketer named in the App. Player names and statistics are used in a factual context.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
